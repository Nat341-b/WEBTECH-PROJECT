📘 Get More Info About Cat Adoption

Adopting a cat is one of the most rewarding decisions you can make. But before you bring your new furry friend home, here's what you need to know to give them the best life possible.

  • Make sure your home is safe and cat-friendly
  • Schedule a vet check-up within the first week
  • Give your cat time to adjust to its new environment
  • Provide proper food, water, and litter box
