Everything you need to know before adopting

🐱 Why Adopt a Cat?

Adopting a cat gives an animal a second chance at a happy life. Shelter and rehomed cats are just as loving and loyal as any other. By adopting, you also help reduce the number of homeless animals in Malaysia.

✅ Adoption Steps

1
Browse Available Cats

Visit our Adopt page to see cats available near you.

2
Click "View Details"

Read the full profile — personality, health, and owner contact.

3
Contact the Owner

Use the contact info in the popup to reach the current owner.

4
Meet the Cat

Arrange a meetup to see if the cat is the right fit for your home.

5
Welcome Home!

Bring your new furry friend home and give them a wonderful life. 🎉

🏠 Preparing Your Home

  • Set up a cozy sleeping area with a soft bed or blanket
  • Place the litter box in a quiet, accessible location
  • Remove toxic plants and secure loose wires
  • Provide scratching posts to protect your furniture
  • Have food, water bowls, and cat toys ready

💉 Health Checklist

  • Schedule a vet visit within the first week
  • Ensure vaccinations are up to date
  • Check for fleas, ticks, and parasites
  • Consider microchipping for identification
  • Ask about spaying or neutering if not already done
